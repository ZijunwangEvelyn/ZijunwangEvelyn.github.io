Notes & Essays

Occasional essays on macroeconomic developments, financial markets, and economic policy, with a focus on China, Hong Kong, and the global economy.

Reading the economy

I will use this space to develop and share my views on the economy as events unfold. Topics will include changes in growth and demand, housing and credit, monetary and fiscal policy, international financial conditions, and the economic implications of AI.

These essays will connect observations from markets and policy to the mechanisms behind them: whose decisions are changing, which constraints matter, and how the effects travel through the economy. They will also make room for questions and interpretations that are still developing, alongside the evidence that could strengthen or challenge them.

New essays will be added periodically.
