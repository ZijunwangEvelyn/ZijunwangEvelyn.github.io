ZIJUN WANG'S WRITING DESK

Notes & Essays

Short, public-facing writing on macroeconomics, finance, policy, and ideas that are still taking shape.

Academic papers demand time and precision. This page has a different purpose: to make one mechanism, one observation, or one policy question intelligible while it is still timely. It is an archive for thinking in public, not a substitute for formal research.

WEEKLY NOTE

One observation

500-800 words. A chart, a news event, a concept, or a piece of evidence - with one clear economic mechanism.

MONTHLY ESSAY

One argument

1,000-1,800 words. A more developed argument connecting a research question to policy or the global economy.

LANGUAGE

Chinese & English

Posts may be in either language, depending on the audience and the question. Each entry will state its language clearly.

Writing Archive

New notes will appear here in reverse chronological order.
